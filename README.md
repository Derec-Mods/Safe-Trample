## About

Ugh, we all know the pain of accidentally jumping on our carefully tilled farmland and ruining the crops in Minecraft. It's incredibly frustrating to constantly have to fix and replant your farm just because of a small misstep or an accidental fall!

This plugin fixes that annoyance with a balanced, vanilla-friendly mechanic: if you are wearing boots equipped with the Feather Falling enchantment, you will no longer trample crops! Your boots are light enough to keep the farmland safe. It's a simple quality of life addition to keep your farms perfectly intact!
